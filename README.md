## The position

**Code is getting cheap. Judgment at the gate is not.**

I will not out-type a frontier model, and I have stopped trying to. The scarce work sits on either side of the keyboard: what gets built, what is allowed to ship, and what evidence a claim must carry before it does. I spend most of my day being wrong faster: running the thing rather than reading it, and finding out which of my own measurements were broken before they reach anyone else.
